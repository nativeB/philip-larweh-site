## What's included

- **Slab decking**: plywood boards on timber joists, propped to level
- **Beam formwork**: beam boxes built and supported along the slab edges and spans
- **Staircase formwork**: built to the shape of the flight
- **Props**: bamboo props spaced to carry the deck and the wet concrete
- **Single-storey and multi-storey work**: the photos below include both

## Installation and materials together

Many builders hire the materials in one place and find the labour somewhere else. With Philip you can do both. He installs the decking and formwork and hires out the [materials for floor decking and formwork](/materials/): [plywood boards](/materials/plywood/), [wawa 2/4 and wawa boards](/materials/wawa/), [bamboo props](/materials/bamboo-props/) and [round column moulds](/materials/column-moulds/). Delivery is available.

Once the deck is up, Philip can also handle the [steel bending](/services/steel-bending/) on top of it.

New to this? Read [how floor decking works](/guides/how-floor-decking-works/).
