## What Philip does

Steel bending is the work of cutting, bending and tying the iron rods (rebar) that sit inside concrete. Philip handles it on site for:

- **Slabs**: top and bottom mesh for suspended floors and decks
- **Beams**: beam cages tied to size and set into the formwork
- **Columns**: column cages and the starter bars left for the next floor
- **Staircases**: reinforcement shaped to follow the flight
- **Sloping slabs and ramps**

The photos on this page are from Philip's own jobs, from single-storey houses to upper floors of multi-storey buildings.

## Steel, formwork and materials in one place

Most slabs need three things done right: the deck, the steel and the props holding it all up. Philip does all three. He installs [formwork and floor decking](/services/formwork-floor-decking/), and he hires out the [materials for floor decking and formwork](/materials/) (plywood, wawa, bamboo props), with delivery available. You deal with one person instead of three.
