## Carpentry Philip does

- **Site carpentry for concrete work**: beam boxes, joists, edge boards and timber supports
- **Staircase formwork**: timber built to the shape of the flight
- **Doors**: made and installed
- **Installation work**: carpentry right through to fitting on site

## Building-site carpentry

On a concrete building, the carpentry decides how straight and solid the finished slab, beams and stairs will be. Philip builds the timber work that holds the concrete in shape: beam boxes, joists, deck edges and stair formwork, propped and ready for the [steel bending](/services/steel-bending/).

If you need the materials too, Philip hires out [wawa 2/4 and wawa boards](/materials/wawa/), [plywood](/materials/plywood/) and [bamboo props](/materials/bamboo-props/).

## Doors and finishing carpentry

Philip also makes doors and installs them. Message him with the number of doors, the sizes and the site location.
