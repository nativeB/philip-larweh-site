## What you can hire

Most of Philip's stock is for **floor decking**, the temporary platform a concrete slab is poured on. It's also used for beam and column formwork.

- **[Plywood boards](/materials/plywood/)**: dark film-faced, red-faced and green film-faced
- **[Wawa 2/4](/materials/wawa/)**: timber for joists and supports
- **[Wawa boards](/materials/wawa/)**: wide rough-sawn boards for formwork
- **[Bamboo props](/materials/bamboo-props/)**: to prop the deck and beams
- **[Round column moulds](/materials/column-moulds/)**: plastic moulds for round columns, to hire or buy

## How hiring works

1. Send Philip the items and rough quantities, your site location and how long you need them.
2. He confirms availability, delivery and the rate.
3. The materials are delivered, or you collect them.

Not sure what you need? Read [how floor decking works](/guides/how-floor-decking-works/), or just send your slab size and Philip will advise.
