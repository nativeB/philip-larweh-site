## Plywood for floor decking and formwork

Plywood forms the surface the concrete is poured onto. On a slab it's laid over timber joists; on beams and columns it forms the sides. Philip hires out:

- **Dark film-faced plywood**: a smooth, coated face that gives a clean concrete finish
- **Red-faced plywood**
- **Green film-faced plywood**

## Hire it with everything else

Plywood is only part of a deck. Philip also hires out [wawa 2/4](/materials/wawa/) for joists and [bamboo props](/materials/bamboo-props/) to hold it up, so you can get the whole set from one place. Delivery is available.

He can also [install the decking](/services/formwork-floor-decking/) for you.
